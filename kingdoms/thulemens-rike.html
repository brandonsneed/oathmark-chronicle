The Striving of Vöde and Wegg
and how Vöde was named Father of Gods and Men

Part 1 — The Murder of Vöde

In the days before the sons of men sought glory, the she-wolf Sep begot two sons, Wegg she begot first and Vöde she begot last. Wegg was mighty in arms and did hunt the mighty beasts that dwelled upon the earth in those days. Vöde was a keeper of sheep herds and singer of songs of great beauty.

One day, Vöde came across the great three-eyed raven, the wisest of all beasts, whose wing had been pierced by the well aimed arrow of his brother Wegg. Vöde spoke to the raven saying thus:

"Hail, King of Wisdom, I see that you have been felled by my great brother, strong in arms. Great Winged-One, I know that you have learned the Runes of Life from the Everlasting Sun, teach this secret to me and I shall save your life this very moment."

And the raven answered him thus:

"Verily, son of the wolf, I will teach you these secret runes if you do save me from this bloody end."

Thus Vöde hid the bird from his brother and did learn the Runes of Life, which were written at the foundations of the world.

When Wegg learned that his brother had stolen the bird away from him, he was possessed by a bloody rage. In his great fury Wegg did swear to the vault of the sky and the bones of the Earth that he would slay his brother.

But knowing that Vöde was clever and might escape him, Wegg went to their mother, the she-wolf, with much venison and mead. Feeding her with this venison and mead, he spake thus to her:

"Honored mother, the day shall soon come when I shall be master of our kin, our aldermen, and our thegns. Behold my mighty stature and taste of the fruits of my labors and know that I am a youth no more. Entrust to me the secret names of mine brother and I that I may receive my inheritance in full."

But she said to her son:

"Nay, it has been since days of old that a mother must never part with the secret names of her kin, lest they fall into the hands of enemies."

And so Wegg bade his mother drink more and become drunk, before asking a second time:

"Honored mother, for love of me and all our kin, whisper into my ear the secret names of our folk, that I may be master of all."

But she said to him:

"Nay, even for love of you and all our kin, I cannot tell you the secret names which I have given to you."

And so Wegg bade his mother drink more and become more drunk, so that her sight began to fail before asking a third time:

"Honored mother, it is your son Vöde, whom you favor greatest of all. For love of me, whisper into my ear mine own secret name."

And at this, the she-wolf Sep, relented to the trickery of her son Wegg and told him the secret name of his brother.

Thereafter, Wegg called upon his brother Vöde by his secret name and Vöde obeyed his brother saying "Here I am!" And so Wegg slew Vöde most cruelly, with his axe he parted him limb from limb and threw the pieces into the waters. The very lifeblood of Vöde he cast into the fire, that it could not cry out for justice.

Therefore the shade of Vöde drifted downwards to the Black Deeps where tears are shed everlasting and dust is eaten for bread.

Part 2 — Harrowing of the Black Deeps

Down into the dread rike, the kingdom of the shades, did the murdered Vöde descend. But having the runes of life in his heart Vöde kept his form amongst the shades and to the devouring beasts which dwell in those reaches he said "depart from me!" and they fled from him.

The Queen of Black named Hekt spied this noble shade which walked her rike lacking fear or sorrow and called to him:

"What manner of shade are thee that you neither cry out in misery nor are you subjected to the diverse torments of my savage thegns!"

And so he answered this Queen:

"Hail, dread sovereign! My name is Vöde, son of Sep the far ranging she-wolf! I met my fate most cruelly at the hands of mine own brother, for greed alone that man embodied rage and took the name of kin-slayer!

By secret arts I keep my name and form and come before thee as stranger and supplicant! Have mercy on this sorry shade and permit me grace thy shadowed courts!"

Moved by the sorry state of the noble Vöde, Hekt brought him into her strongholds and before her and her black aldermen he sang the songs of which the gods of heaven and the gods of earth do sing. And so Hekt came to love him, and Vöde knew her, and from her learned the many spells that have lain hidden from the sun and from the moon and from the countless stars since the foundation of the world.

To the deepest reaches of all the world Vöde was brought, to a place where all the gore from every battlefield and every wild hunt runs and pools together amid the roots of a great black oak that lay in the heart of the Black Deeps. With the Runes of Life and the Runes of Death, Vöde engraved a secret word onto the bark of the black oak, and immediately a branch fell from the oak and formed itself into a black stave that Vöde did take up.

Upon the black stave was written the secret names of every god, every spirit, every man, and every beast that has lived or will live. With every secret and every art that Vöde had learned, he found the secret way out of the Black Deeps.

Walking forwards and walking backwards he passed the twelve black gates with their twelve passwords and one hundred and forty four judges.

But the Black Queen Hekt pursued Vöde, for she did love him, but he did not look back at her, only saying:

"I dare not turn back, lest I be tempted by beauty to abandon my fate which is with the living."

And so Vöde stepped out beneath the sun once more, and commanded that a stone seal up the way to the Black Deeps for all time.

Thereafter, Vöde spoke to the waters saying:

"Waters! Give up my flesh and bones."

And the waters obeyed.

Then he spoke to the fire:

"Fire! Give up my blood."

And the fire obeyed.

Made whole once more, Vöde called upon his brother Wegg by his secret name, and he knelt before Vöde in supplication.

"Brother! Seek not mine life in vengeance, but have mercy on I who was merciless!"

And Vöde said to him:

"Your life shall not be mine, foolish brother, but your eyes and tongue I shall take from you. For none shall utter my secret name again, and for all time you will be god and master of those who, like you, were struck dumb and blind by anger and the seeking for blood. Upon countless battlefields will you descend, inspiring the fury of those gore mad warriors."

And so was it done to Wegg, that blood mad god.

Thus did the younger son enslave the elder, and then Vöde, god of runes and song and secrets, set about his work.

Oaths of everlasting loyalty did he impose on every god on earth and in heaven.
He commanded that the earth should be solid and unmoving.
He tamed the seas that they should be navigable.
He raised up mighty Thul from the sea and placed there a noble people.
He built his shining halls above the Everlasting Sun.
He took shining Frik as his wife, and from them proceeded the generations of the gods.

And so did Vöde order heaven and earth, and all gods and men sayeth to him forever:

"Hail, All-Father!"
