A fuller history of Iceholme — its rulers, halls, and holdings — has yet to be set down. This page will grow as the chronicle does.

Ruler & Notable Persons

  • Iceholme's rulerNot yet named or titled. Parent to Prince Bjorn.
  • Prince BjornA prince of Iceholme, sent with his own guard to broker an alliance with Innsjøkongen, the Lake King (Battle 5).
  • GydaA young völva, pursuing a vision that called her toward water. Wounded in Battle 5 and carried from the field by the artillery crew she had been protecting alongside.
  • The HersirCommanded the axemen defending the artillery and Gyda against a cavalry charge in Battle 5. Title only — not yet named.
  • Innsjøkongen, the Lake KingAn Eachy, ruler of an isolated lake community. Allied to Iceholme since Battle 5, in exchange for the lake's preservation and a regular tithe of food. Not himself a dwarf.
  • The dwarf captainLed the border skirmish at the Fountain Crossroads (Battle 1). Remains unnamed.
