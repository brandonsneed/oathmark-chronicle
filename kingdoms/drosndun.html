Drosndun

Orcs

A fuller history of Drosndun — its Tyrant, its tribes, and its holdings — has yet to be set down. This page will grow as the chronicle does.

The orcs of Drosndun do not call these lands by any name the other kingdoms use. In their own tongue, it is Herkland — after Herkir, the noble bloodline the High Tyrant claims descent from — and theirs by a right older than any of the surrounding crowns.
