Ruler & Notable Persons

  • An ancient sorcerer-kingSemi-divine, out of the Sidhean's mythic past. Entombed beside the river Aln for untold centuries until roused from rest this spring, dismayed to find his resting place settled by human farms (Battle 4). His name is not recorded.
